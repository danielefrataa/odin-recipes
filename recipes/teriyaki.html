Teriyaki Chicken

Description

Ingredients

  1. 1 pound lean ground beef
  2. 1 onion, chopped
  3. 2 cloves garlic, minced
  4. 1 (28 ounce) can crushed tomatoes
  5. 2 (6 ounce) cans tomato paste
  6. 2 (6.5 ounce) cans canned tomato sauce
  7. 1/2 cup water
  8. 2 tablespoons white sugar

Steps

  • Gather all ingredients
  • In a large skillet over medium heat, cook and stir ground beef until brown. Drain excess fat. Add onion and garlic; cook until onion is tender. Stir in crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, tomato sauce, and water. Season with sugar, basil, fennel seeds, Italian seasoning, 1 tablespoon salt, pepper, and 2 tablespoons parsley. Simmer, covered, for about 1 1/2 hours, stirring occasionally.
  • Bring a large pot of lightly salted water to a boil. Cook lasagna noodles in boiling water for 8 to 10 minutes. Drain noodles, and rinse with cold water. In a mixing bowl, combine ricotta cheese with egg, remaining parsley, and 1/2 teaspoon salt.
